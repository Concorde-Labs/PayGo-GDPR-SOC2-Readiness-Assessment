# PayGo Financial Technologies Ltd. | GDPR & SOC 2 Readiness Assessment

**Industry:** Financial Technology (Fintech)  
**Assessment Focus:** Data Privacy, Information Security & Compliance Readiness  
**Frameworks:** EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) | SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria

## Project Overview

PayGo Financial Technologies Ltd. is a fictional Dublin-based fintech organization providing digital payment services to individuals and small businesses across Ireland and other EU countries. Its operations involve processing customer, employee, transaction, and security-related information through internal systems, cloud infrastructure, and external service providers.

The assessment examines PayGo's personal data processing practices against relevant GDPR requirements and evaluates its readiness for a potential SOC 2 examination.

The review covers seven personal data processing activities and assesses information security controls against three SOC 2 Trust Services Categories: Security, Availability, and Confidentiality.

## Assessment Objectives

- Develop a personal data inventory covering processing activities, data categories, data subjects, systems, recipients, and retention periods.
- Document personal data flows and identify information requiring verification.
- Assess relevant GDPR requirements and identify data protection gaps.
- Evaluate security controls and available evidence against applicable SOC 2 criteria.
- Develop a SOC 2 evidence register to support control verification and audit readiness.
- Consolidate privacy and security findings into a risk-based remediation plan.

## Key Assessment Findings

| Assessment Area | Key Findings |
|---|---|
| Data Governance | Incomplete records of processing activities and gaps in privacy notice disclosures |
| Data Retention | Incomplete retention justifications and insufficient deletion testing across systems and external providers |
| Access Management | Incomplete role-permission mapping and missing periodic access reviews for identified systems |
| Authentication & Monitoring | Undocumented MFA coverage and insufficient evidence of timely critical alert reviews |
| Incident Response & Recovery | Incident response procedures not tested within the past 12 months and backup restoration testing not evidenced |
| Change Management | Inconsistent documentation of change approvals and pre-deployment testing |
| Third-Party Risk Management | Incomplete provider risk assessments, monitoring, and documentation of data protection responsibilities |

The assessment distinguishes identified weaknesses from areas where additional evidence is needed to determine control effectiveness or compliance status.
